## Current Runtime Behavior

Prices:

- known laboratory and diagnostic prices are answered deterministically by the server before Hermes
- exact service names win over similar names
- common diagnostic aliases are handled:
  - `EKQ` -> `Ürəyin elektrokardioqramması`
  - `EEQ` -> `Elektroensefoloqramma (EEQ)`
  - `EXO` -> `Rəngli ЕХО dopplerokardioqrafiya`
- booking direction remains `Qəbul`

Safety and fallback:

- preparation questions route to operator because no confirmed preparation document exists yet
- unavailable services such as `MRT` and `Stomatologiya` are not confirmed and route to `WhatsApp` / `Telegram`
- medication/treatment questions are refused deterministically and route to a doctor/`Qəbul`
- home visit remains not confirmed
- branch and reception-time answers remain deterministic

Doctors:

- doctor data is present in the structured knowledge context
- doctor answers are still generated by Hermes from the context
- next recommended improvement is deterministic doctor/specialty lookup, similar to price lookup
- direct doctor phone numbers must not be exposed in Dr. Dia answers

Contacts and hours:

- contacts and hours still come from CMS/settings
- branch is treated as one branch according to current product direction
